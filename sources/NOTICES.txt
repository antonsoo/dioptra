The eight displayed Greek selections adapt that digital transcription.
Changes: ordinary Greek delta Δ replaces the transcription's ∠; line-end
hyphens and whitespace are joined; line numbers and apparatus are omitted
from the selections; apostrophes are normalized; editorial supplements use
visible angle brackets. The source-file copy itself is unchanged.
