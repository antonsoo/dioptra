## Design Context

### Users

English-speaking learners interested in ancient Greek engineering, geometry and
the original language. The creator asks for substantial, distinctive experiments
that teach something surprising, with illustrations and inspectable sources.
They explicitly reject generic showcase catalogues and superficial additions.

### Brand Personality

Observant, exact, curious. A survey field book in which a learner can make and
correct a consequential geometric choice. Source authority stays visible without
pretending that a mathematical construction reconstructs a particular ancient site.

### Aesthetic Direction

Chosen for this experiment: pale blue survey paper, deep ink blue, restrained
ochre for the intended line and vermilion for a displaced aiming point. Large
serif propositions and plain humanist control text. One spacious drawing with
an adjacent signed-distance ledger, not a dashboard or a grid of cards.

### Design Principles

1. Let a measured action reveal the theorem before explaining the algebra.
2. Keep the mountain schematic and the coordinates honest; both axes use equal scale.
3. Attach Greek phrases to the action they describe and preserve printed evidence.
4. Design a full upright view for a phone, with keyboard equivalents for every action.
5. A saved study must retain its drawings, sources, language and licensing offline.
